Help & venue guidelines

For students

  • Browse events on the Events page and filter by category, date, price or availability.
  • Free events: choose Register. Ticketed events use a simulated checkout; no real money is taken in this prototype.
  • Your ticket code is emailed to you, and sent on WhatsApp if you opt in under My account.
  • Follow clubs and groups and pick interests to get better recommendations.

For organizers

  1. Create a draft event from the Organizer dashboard.
  2. Check the venue calendar, then choose Request venue. You'll be told straight away if your time overlaps another booking.
  3. Attach a signed faculty or Student Affairs approval letter (PDF or Word, up to 5 MB).
  4. An administrator approves or rejects the request. Approved events are published immediately.

Venue use rules

  • Bookings are confirmed only after administrator approval. Authorised university staff make the final decision.
  • Expected attendance must not exceed the venue's capacity.
  • Leave the venue as you found it, and finish on time so the next booking can start.

Need more help?

Contact the Student Affairs office on weekdays between 08:00 and 16:30.
